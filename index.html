Ibukun Keyamo

Nigerian journalist and public relations specialist

Also published as Ibukun Crystal Keyamo

Ibukun Keyamo is a Nigerian journalist and public relations specialist. She is a Senior Journalist at Baden Bower, and her bylines have run in Forbes Georgia, OK! Magazine, International Business Times, The Village Voice, TechTimes and TechAcute.

About

Ibukun writes about business, technology, entrepreneurship, culture and media. Much of her recent work profiles founders and the companies they run: an AI security firm putting autonomous robots on patrol, a Dubai company building an Arabic-first voice assistant, an engineer winning hackathons at a Bay Area AI lab.

The work she cares about most is explanation. A pricing system, a funding model or a piece of enterprise software can usually be written about so that a general reader follows it, and she treats that as the job. The same habit carries into her public relations work, where she helps brands, organisations and individuals say clearly what they do.

Her background also covers editing, copywriting, content strategy and search engine optimisation. She is based in Nigeria.
